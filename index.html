02 — Using the machine

Brew it, enjoy it.

Short version for when you're standing at the counter.

  1. Power on

    Flip the switch on the right and wait for the green light (~3 min).

  2. Add water

    Fill the carafe to the line, pour into the back reservoir.

  3. Load grounds

    One scoop per cup into a fresh filter. Beans live in the cabinet above.

  4. Brew

    Press START. A full pot takes about 6 minutes.

Running low? Beans, filters, and milk live in the cabinet above the machine. If a tin is down to the last scoop, grab a backup from the storage closet — or post in #coffee so someone on a supply run can restock.

03 — Good to know

A few quick answers.

How does the coffee fund work?

The station is paid for by the people who drink from it. Contributions go into one shared PayPal Pool that buys beans, filters, milk, and the occasional new gadget. It's voluntary — chip in whatever feels fair for how much you drink.

Where do I see the current balance?

On the PayPal Pool page — it's the single source of truth. Use the "Open the PayPal Pool" button above.

How are coffee purchases announced?

Every purchase gets a quick note in #coffee on Slack, so everyone can see where the money goes.

Is this an official fundraiser?

No. It's an informal, voluntary pool run by the people who drink the coffee, and it isn't affiliated with any company or charity.
